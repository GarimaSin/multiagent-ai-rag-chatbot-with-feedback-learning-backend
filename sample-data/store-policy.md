# Sample store policy — fictional demonstration data

Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase. A receipt is required for all refunds.

Annual plans include email support. The standard response target for email support is two business days.

Subscriptions can be cancelled from the account settings page. Cancellation stops the next renewal; it does not automatically issue a refund.

This is sample data for testing a chatbot, not the policy of any real company.
